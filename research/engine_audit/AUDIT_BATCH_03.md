## Import decision

The calendar-spread candidate is the only new futures mechanism counted in this batch. It is not yet wired into the generic candle runner: a valid test needs synchronized dated-contract curves and a contract-aware two-leg P&L model. Sending it through the current single-symbol M1 stop/target adapter would change the strategy and create misleading results. The MIT licence allows copying with attribution, but the full upstream package is not copied until its data and accounting contract is implemented.
