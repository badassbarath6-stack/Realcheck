## Deduplication and rejection from the inspected source pool

- The four TSMOM implementations are one time-series momentum family; different horizons, volatility scaling and adaptive lookbacks are variants, not four unique mechanisms.
- The funding-carry volatility adjustment, funding-momentum filter, high-beta funding carry and multi-funding filter are overlays/variants of funding carry and are not counted separately.
- The OU BTC/ETH ratio and BTC/ETH ratio RV implementations are the same pair-spread family. They also do not actually construct a two-leg executable portfolio in the evaluator output; the single-asset proxy must not be called a full pairs backtest.
- Donchian and ATR breakout are channel-breakout variants; not counted as separate engines. The prior compression breakout candidate remains held pending a stricter comparison.
- Dual EMA crossover and KAMA are trend-following variants. The BTC/ETH/alt lead-lag timeframe variants, multi-lag ensemble and impulse catch-up are one cross-asset lead-lag family; do not count each lag or asset leader separately.
- Residual mean reversion and residual RSI regime are variants of beta-adjusted mean reversion. High-beta funding carry is a funding-carry overlay.
- Novel-state trend overlaps the existing volatility-expansion/momentum family. Liquidation-cascade source uses extreme funding plus volatility expansion but no liquidation feed; it is not a true liquidation detector and is deduplicated with the funding/volatility family.
- Funding-dispersion overlay and funding-surface regime share the same cross-asset funding-surface inputs; only the distinct cross-sectional funding-surface regime is counted.
- The cross-sectional “momentum” source ranks cumulative funding rather than asset price returns and contains contradictory comments about the meaning of funding rank; reject as mislabeled/ambiguous.
- The Renko source initializes brick size with the mean ATR of the entire input dataframe, leaking future information into early bricks; reject until the initial brick size is causal. Its output also drops bars with no brick and requires a dedicated event/brick simulator.
- The Elliott V2 file has a populate_indicators function outside the strategy class in the inspected source; reject as an executable strategy import.
- The Almgren–Chriss sample has generic RSI entries and a separate execution-slicing algorithm; count the execution algorithm only as execution infrastructure, not as an independent directional alpha engine. The market-making family is already represented by the Avellaneda–Stoikov candidate.
- EMA/ADX/OTT/Supertrend/Alligator/Ichimoku/MA-ribbon strategies are conventional trend-indicator variants; they are not each counted as unique mechanisms. Ichimoku cloud structure remains a future candidate only if a later audit establishes a genuinely distinct cloud-regime entry/exit hypothesis.
- Keltner breakout is a volatility-envelope breakout variant. ClucMay72018, CofiBit, SwingHighToSky and similar oscillator/BB pullback variants are not separately counted from the already audited price mean-reversion family.
