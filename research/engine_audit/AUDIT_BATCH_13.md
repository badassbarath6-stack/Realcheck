## New distinct mechanisms accepted conditionally

| Mechanism | Inspected source | Exact signal | Decision / blockers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stablecoin supply growth as latent risk-asset demand | [09_etf_stablecoin_flows.py](https://github.com/mikemarshall01/crypto-quant-strategies/blob/main/notebooks/09_etf_stablecoin_flows.py) | Pull total USD-pegged stablecoin supply from DeFiLlama; compute the 7-calendar-day percentage change and lag it by one day. The notebook selects the stronger multi-day IC between this signal and its ETF-flow candidate, sign-adjusts it, then holds BTC long when the signal is above its trailing 60-day median and otherwise holds cash; position is shifted one day and a 10 bps cost is charged on each change. | CONDITIONAL PASS as a slow macro/liquidity-regime filter, not a standalone directional futures alpha. Real DeFiLlama data is available, but aggregate supply mixes exchange on-ramps, DeFi, remittances and arbitrage flows. The notebook selects the stronger of two signals and chooses sign using the same sample later backtested, so the result is exploratory and selection-biased without a separate holdout. Needs point-in-time supply publication timestamps and a multi-asset/perp test. Repo MIT licence; no code copied. |
| Cross-sectional on-chain exchange netflow factor | [netflow_probe.py](https://github.com/BlackLotus0930/crypto-quant-research/blob/main/archive/info-alpha/netflow_probe.py) | Aggregate Dune ERC-20 exchange inflow/outflow USD by asset/day; roll the netflow over seven days; lag the completed daily observation by 24 hours before aligning it to the hourly point-in-time perp panel. At each timestamp, rank the negative netflow cross-sectionally (net outflow / accumulation ranks long; net inflow / potential sell pressure ranks short), normalize the book, smooth weights and rebalance every 24 bars with a 30% step and 1% deadband. The source charges 5 bps per unit turnover and compares signal IC to funding-factor IC. | CONDITIONAL PASS as a distinct on-chain exchange-flow factor. It differs from stablecoin supply growth: it measures token-specific exchange-bound flow, not aggregate stablecoin float. The code explicitly delays the daily flow until the next UTC day, which is the right causal principle. No independently reproducible result table was included in the inspected source, the Dune ERC-20→CEX coverage and token mapping need validation, and the archive has no declared licence. Do not copy code or promote until data provenance, entity mapping, delisted assets and OOS net returns are independently verified. |
